# R23 offline custody design

The user approved offline custody plus event settlement on 2026-10-29: growth continues, offline care mistakes do not increase, and returning after two days must not replay 172,800 seconds.

## Behavior
- R23 uses offline custody by default. The original native catch-up remains an explicit alternative; unsupported BINs are never admitted by filename or shared address guesses.
- Use audited native care/minute and lifecycle paths, skipping idle frame rendering. Evolution, sleep and growth must actually commit, not just edit age or the displayed clock.
- Custody supplies basic care before an unattended timeout. Existing care mistakes, battle/training achievements and pre-existing lifecycle conditions are not erased. Native lifecycle resets remain native.
- New offline punishments caused by absent feeding/cleaning/lights are prevented. Existing illness/death requires a deliberate safe boundary, not fabricated healing or resurrection.
- Waiting for settlement adds no new care debt. Cancellation/reload resumes only remaining committed work, without duplicate growth or lost elapsed time.
- Full native foreground gameplay, QR battle/Jogres, sound and BIN bytes remain unchanged.

## Approaches
The user chose custody/event settlement over offline pause, because growth should continue. Unbounded per-second replay and blindly jumping the clock were rejected: one grows with elapsed time, the other previously lost evolution.
